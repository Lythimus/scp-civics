# Other custodians

Every body below keeps its own records, so a request to the parish doesn't reach them. Which offices are outside parish government, and why, is in `scp-civics:scp-government` (`references/structure.md`).

Contacts are from each body's own page or the state's boards directory, read on the date shown. Confirm the channel before drafting a delivery method. Where no custodian is named, address the request to "Custodian of Public Records" at the body's address and ask in the drafting notes that the office confirm it. Don't guess an email address.

## Anything else

For a body not listed, find its custodian on its own site or by phone. If it can't be identified, leave a bracketed placeholder and say so. Don't guess an address.

For land records, `bayou:eclerks-land-index` searches the Clerk's index; without bayou, use the Clerk's office or eClerks LA directly.

State and federal bodies are outside this skill. `bayou:la-public-records-request` covers state agencies, and federal agencies use FOIA. Without bayou, take a state agency's custodian from its own site and pull the statute as in Step 4 of the skill.
