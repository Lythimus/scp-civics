## What to expect

This is general guidance, not parish policy.

- Notices come from a `request.justfoia.com` address, and replying to a notice reaches the request.
- The parish can open a subsidiary request, for example to run an email search separately.
- Records can be redacted, with labels such as `Client Privilege` and `Privacy Information`.
- Productions can include native `.msg` files and PDFs with no text layer.
- More records can follow a "complete" notice, particularly after a written follow-up that names what is missing.
