# St. Charles Parish Industrial Development Board (IDB)

Full name: Industrial Development Board of the Parish of St. Charles, Louisiana, Inc. A nonprofit corporation with seven directors appointed by the Parish Council. It handles PILOT leases and conduit revenue bonds, meets a few times a year at 11:30 AM, and holds its own files.

**What is established (as of 2026-11-03):**
- The IDB adopted the Council's Public Records Fee Schedule as its own (Res. 2026-5, 10/26/2026 agenda). That confirms it treats itself as a separate custodian.
- Its agenda contact block is the Council's: Courthouse, 15045 Highway 18 (parish pages also say River Road), P.O. Box 302, Hahnville, LA 70057; 985-783-5125 (Council Secretary's line).
- Web search found no IDB-specific records contact, phone, email, web page or portal. Legistar lists its meetings only from 2025-12-22. As of 2026-11-03, signed minutes with adopted resolutions were attached to the meeting pages for 12/22/2025 and 4/23/2026, and not yet for 7/27 or 10/26/2026; the Legistar API returns none of them. Earlier records, leases and the applicant's file have to be requested. `scp-civics:scp-incentives` lists what is public.

**Routing, as told by the Council Secretary and the board's president (reported by a requester, 2026-11-03; confirm before relying on it):**
- The board has no office of its own. The Council office holds its records, but handles only the agendas and minutes.
- **Minutes and agendas:** request them from the Council Secretary (Michelle Impastato, 985-783-5125, mimpastato@stcharlesgov.net) or through the parish records portal.
- **Anything else** (applicant files, leases, bond documents, correspondence): the board's president accepts a request by email and will work with the Council office to locate the records. He serves in the Assessor's Office (985-783-6281). His email address is on the Assessor's domain (stcharlesassessor.com) and was given by the requester; confirm it against the Assessor's site or by phone if a message bounces. A bounce is not a response.
- The president is an individual board officer, not a records custodian with a published process. Keep a written request on file with the Council office as well, so there is a dated record in the parish's own system.

**What is NOT established:** who the IDB's designated records custodian is, and whether special counsel or bond counsel holds board files (lease drafts, correspondence) outside the Council office. Do not tell the user either is confirmed.

**Which items go where:**
- Council office or portal: adopted resolutions with exhibits, minutes (draft minutes of unapproved meetings), any Council resolution or ordinance consenting to the board's financing (Article XIII of the board's articles; a Council record), anything else the Council office has filed for the board.
- The board's president: the applicant's proposal or request, lease and bond agreements and drafts, the hiring-policy statement and applicant's commitment (Code Sec. 19-16(b)), correspondence with the applicant or special counsel. Ask also that he search whatever email or accounts he uses for board business.
- Clerk of Court: a recorded act of conveyance, once recorded. It need not be requested if the index shows it.

**Recommended path:**
1. Send the president one email with the substantive items, copying the Council Secretary and asking that the Council office search its own files for the same items. Ask for written confirmation of receipt and a date for production.
2. Send the minutes and resolutions item to the Council Secretary or the portal at the same time.
3. If the two answers diverge, or no written acknowledgment arrives in 3 business days (confirm the figure against the current R.S. 44:32 text, as the Step 4 statute pull does), follow up by phone and log it.
4. Do not rely on a verbal commitment to "work with the Council office": a dated email request is what starts the statutory clock. Say so in the cover note.
5. Where the president is also a relative or associate of a party in a related matter, say nothing about it in the request; note it in the private drafting notes only.
