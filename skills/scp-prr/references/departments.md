## Choosing

- Tick the departments that would hold the record, usually two to seven. A wider set gives more offices to search. Whether the number of boxes changes turnaround has not been shown: one 15-box request with a narrow subject and named accounts still came back in days.
- Add the department that wrote or received the record, not only the one that decided the matter.
- Tick Legal only when attorney records are wanted or Legal is a likely party to the correspondence.

Examples to adapt:

| Subject | Departments |
|---|---|
| Rezoning or subdivision | Planning & Zoning; Council Office; Public Works/Engineering; add Administration or Economic Development if they were involved |
| Drainage project | Public Works/Engineering; Grants; Finance; GIS |
| Contract or purchase | Procurement; Finance; Contract Monitor |
| Grant-funded project | Grants; Finance; the department running the project |
| Utility service to a site | Wastewater; Waterworks; Planning & Zoning |
