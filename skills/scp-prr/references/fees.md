# Parish fees

These apply to parish government only. The Sheriff's Office charges $1 per page; see `custodians/scpso.md`.

As of 2026-11-02, from Ord. 26-8-12 (Legistar file 2026-0263), approved 2026-08-24, effective immediately, amending Code of Ordinances Sec. 2-2. Read from the signed ordinance. Check Legistar for later amendments before relying on this.

**The parish page's FEES link still opened the superseded Ord. 25-12-14 on 2026-11-02.** That version charged $0.25 per page for emails and $5 per 100 pages for other electronic documents. Those charges were struck. If a custodian or the page quotes them, cite Ord. 26-8-12.

## Free

Viewing any document, and these electronic copies:

- Miscellaneous documents
- Emails (the earlier email fee row was struck entirely)
- Code of Ordinances, its supplements, and the Zoning and Subdivision appendices
- Bid specifications and bid results
- Adopted long-range plans
- Audited and unaudited financial statements

## Waivers

The council or the parish president may, in the public interest, waive the charge for copies of an ordinance, motion, resolution or other record of a council proceeding. The council secretary may waive it for a governing body, public official, parish board or agency, or public bidder. These words do not cover every parish record.

The Industrial Development Board adopted the Council's schedule by its own resolution (Res. 2026-5, 10/26/2026 agenda). Whether it tracks later amendments has not been checked. Other custodians set their own fees.
