# Rules for every custodian

Day counts and wording as of 2026-11-02. Re-pull the current statute before quoting them.

## Law

- Each public body that keeps its own files is its own custodian. A request to the parish does not reach a board, agency or office that keeps separate records.
- A record not in active use must be presented immediately. If it is in active use, the custodian certifies that in writing and sets a day and hour within three days (R.S. 44:33(B)).
- If the custodian questions whether a record is public, it owes a written determination within five days (R.S. 44:32(D), amended by Acts 2024, No. 411; older templates still say three).
- Both counts exclude Saturdays, Sundays and legal public holidays. R.S. 44:35 covers the remedy for no response, which needs a lawyer. This skill does not draft pleadings.
- There is no fee to examine a record (R.S. 44:32(C)(3)). Ask to cite the exemption for anything withheld, and for the rest of the record.
- The custodian may ask about the specificity of the request, but not why the requester wants the records. Do not state a purpose unless it helps, as a hearing date does.
- The Act reaches existing records. It does not make a custodian answer questions or create a document, so ask for records, not explanations.

## Practice

- A request can alert the subject of the records that someone is looking.
- Keep the sent copy and proof of sending (portal confirmation, sent-email header, certified-mail receipt) beside the draft, and record the send date and tracking number in the project's notes.
- Name records already produced under an earlier request so the custodian does not produce them again.

## After sending

- **Check the production against each numbered item.** A "complete" notice means the custodian considers it finished, not that everything was found.
- **To follow up,** reply to the notification or write to the custodian, citing the original request number and date. List each missing record and the produced record that shows it exists. Ask for production or a written basis for withholding. Do not ask questions, and do not argue.
- **Look for a split.** A custodian can open a subsidiary request, so one request becomes two numbers with separate productions. Track both.
- **Compare overlapping copies.** Email searches can return several renderings of the same thread with different redactions, and sometimes a native message file with headers.
- **Treat other people's details as private.** Productions can include other requesters' requests. Do not republish their contact details.
