# scp-prr requester profile (example)

Copy this somewhere outside the plugin and any public repository, fill it in, and point `SCP_CIVICS_PROFILE` at the copy. The skill reads it for drafts and shows the values before entering them on a form.

The parish form requires only name, phone and email. Requests and form details can resurface in records produced to other people, so consider a dedicated email address, and leave out what you would not want repeated.
